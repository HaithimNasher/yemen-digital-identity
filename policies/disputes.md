# Domain Dispute Policy — Draft

Status: `DRAFT`

## Scope

The policy architecture must support disputes involving:
- conflicting rights to a domain name
- trademarks or protected names
- impersonation
- bad-faith registration
- public-institution names
- registrar conduct

## Principles

- neutral process
- documented notice
- response opportunity
- evidence preservation
- independent decision pathway
- transparent status tracking
- registry enforcement only after a valid decision or authorized process

YDII does not make legal findings through the software prototype.
