# Abuse Management Policy — Draft

Status: `DRAFT`

## Abuse categories

Potential categories include:
- phishing
- malware distribution
- botnet command-and-control
- credential theft
- DNS abuse
- impersonation
- malicious redirection
- nameserver compromise
- fraudulent registrar activity

## Intake

Every report should receive:
- case ID
- timestamp
- reporter contact channel
- affected domain(s)
- evidence references
- severity classification
- assigned owner
- status

## Response states

```text
RECEIVED
TRIAGED
INVESTIGATING
ACTION_REQUIRED
MONITORING
RESOLVED
CLOSED
```

## Registry actions

Any suspension or registry lock must:
- follow adopted policy
- identify authority and reason
- be auditable
- be reversible when policy permits
- preserve evidence

Emergency controls must be narrowly scoped and logged.
