# Qatar — `.qa`

## Model
Qatar provides `.qa` direct registration plus sector zones such as `.com.qa`, `.name.qa`, `.edu.qa`, `.sch.qa`, `.gov.qa` and others.

## Relevant design patterns for YDII
- open national zone
- personal namespace
- documentary eligibility for restricted sectors
- accredited registrars plus registry-direct restricted zones
- Arabic `.قطر`
