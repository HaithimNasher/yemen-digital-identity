# Kuwait — `.kw`

## Model
Kuwait CITRA publishes an extensive policy library and operates through accredited registrars. Current FAQ lists `.com.kw`, `.net.kw`, `.org.kw`, `.edu.kw`, `.gov.kw`, `.ind.kw`.

## Relevant design patterns for YDII
- registration bylaws
- WHOIS/privacy policy
- expiry and renewal policy
- reserved/blocked names policy
- complaints policy
- registrar accreditation
